AC repair in this part of town

The repair we are called out for most in Overland Park is a house that cools the front rooms and never quite reaches the back. In the 1970s stock north of 95th that is almost always the return ducting rather than the condenser, and a bigger unit makes it worse rather than better. We check static pressure before quoting anything, which occasionally turns a replacement quote into a much cheaper piece of duct work.

South of 151st the usual call is short cycling in a two-storey build with a single-stage system. That is a sizing problem, not a fault, and it is worth knowing before you pay to repair a compressor that is going to fail again.
