What we see in Overland Park

A lot of the housing stock north of 95th went up between 1965 and 1985, and a good share of it is still on the ductwork it was built with. The systems have been replaced two or three times; the returns have not.

That shows up as a house that cools unevenly — the back bedrooms never quite get there — and it is worth checking before anyone talks about a bigger unit. We measure static pressure first, which now and then turns a replacement quote into a much cheaper piece of duct work.

Newer builds south of 151st have the opposite problem. Tight envelopes and two-storey volumes make a single-stage system short-cycle, which wears the compressor out early. A two-stage or variable-speed replacement pays for itself there in a way it would not in an older ranch.

Summers here run humid enough that a system sized purely on square footage will cool the air and leave the house feeling damp. Getting the run time long enough to pull moisture out matters more in Johnson County than raw capacity does, which is the argument for two-stage equipment in almost every house we quote.

Winters are the other half of it. A heat pump alone will not carry a January night here, so anything we fit is either dual-fuel or backed by a furnace we have looked at. We will not sell a system that leaves you on emergency heat for a week.

Overland Park inspects replacements, and the permit is ours to pull and schedule. It adds nothing to your day and about a week to the paperwork, and we would rather tell you that up front than have it arrive as a surprise.

Overland Park questions

Do you cover all of Overland Park?

Yes, from 63rd down to 179th, and everything either side of Metcalf.

Is there a travel charge?

No. The diagnostic is the same $89 wherever you are in Johnson County.

Do you pull permits here?

Yes, for any replacement. Overland Park inspects, and we handle the scheduling.

How soon can you get here?

Most repair calls the same day, and we give you the window when you call.

Do you service apartments and condos?

Yes, where the unit is the owner's responsibility. Tell us the building when you call and we will say what access we need.

Can you match what a neighbour had fitted?

Often, yes. Give us the address or the model and we will tell you whether the same equipment suits your house.
