03How it works

Three steps, no surprises

1

You call and we book a window

We ask what the system is doing — whether it is blowing warm air, making a noise, or not turning on at all — and a few questions to get a sense of the fault before anyone is dispatched. That is what lets the technician arrive with the right parts on the van.

2

A technician diagnoses it in front of you

We check the outdoor condenser, the indoor air handler, the thermostat settings and the refrigerant levels to find the root cause rather than the symptom. You are walked through what was found, and shown the part if there is one to see.

3

You get the price, then you decide

The repair is quoted before anything is touched, in plain terms rather than technical jargon, along with what each option involves. Nothing happens until you say so, and the number does not move once the work is underway.

06Before you call

The questions we get most

Do you charge for a diagnosis?+

There is an $89 call-out charge for a diagnosis, and it comes off the bill if you go ahead with the repair on the same visit.

How soon can someone come out?+

Most repair calls are seen the same or the next working day. If the house has no heat we treat it as urgent.

Do you work on any make?+

Yes. Our technicians carry parts for the makes common in Olathe homes, and we order anything else the same day.

Is the estimate free on a replacement?+

Yes. Estimates on replacements and new installations are free, including the sizing calculation for the house.

Do you offer financing?+

Financing is available on system replacements. We will go through the options with you before anything is ordered.

Who actually turns up at the house?+

One of our own technicians. We do not subcontract, and the person who diagnoses the fault is the person who repairs it.
